Photo Viewer Project

Interactive Slideshow

Explore the gallery, open images in full view, and save your top picks in a favorites section using DOM functionality.

How to Use

  • Click the left and right arrows to move through the gallery.
  • Click the play button to start or pause automatic slideshow mode.
  • Click any image to open it in full-size view.
  • Use the Add to Favorites button in the lightbox window.
  • A maximum of 5 images can be saved in favorites.
